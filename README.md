## Company Overview

Everstage is a SaaS platform that helps B2B companies manage their sales pipeline, forecast revenue and optimize their sales process. It is designed for RevOps, Finance and Sales leaders, and its CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) module helps ensure revenue is profitable, strategic and aligned with company goals.

## Objectives

**Primary objective:** automate and optimize incentive compensation management (ICM) through accurate commission calculations, real-time transparency for sales teams, and incentives aligned with revenue growth.

**Secondary objectives:**
- Enhance sales performance
- Enable data-driven decision making
- Eliminate manual errors
- Improve operational efficiency
- Strengthen transparency and trust
- Support effective sales planning

## Technology Initiatives

- **AI-powered revenue platform:** combines incentives, CPQ and planning in one system
- **End-to-end commission automation:** calculation, validation and payout, reducing over- and underpayment errors
- **No-code plan builder and scenario simulation:** includes a "Time Machine" feature to simulate outcomes
- **Real-time visibility:** reps can track earnings and performance instantly, with forecasting and payout visibility
- **Advanced data integration:** connects with CRM, ERP and HRIS systems and handles large enterprise data volumes
- **AI-driven dealrooms and CPQ:** interactive dealrooms with AI-suggested pricing, configurations and deal structures

## Industry Category and Product Mix

- **Primary industry:** SaaS (Software as a Service)
- **Sub-industry:** Sales Performance Management (SPM) and Incentive Compensation Management (ICM)
- **Tools built:** ICM, CRM and data integration, reporting and analytics engine, workflow automation and plan builder, commission visibility portal (rep view)

**Pricing model:** subscription-based with multiple tiers by number of users and features, typically customized to organization needs, with optional add-on modules.

**Product pillars:**
- **Incentives (commission automation):** commission plans, real-time tracking, gamified insights, payout automation, audit trail, earnings forecast
- **Planning (quota and performance):** quota management, territory planning, performance analytics, team dashboards, revenue insights, ROI analysis
- **CPQ (AI-first quoting):** AI quote generation, pricing guardrails, digital sales rooms, e-signature (DocuSign), quote-to-commission, Slack integration

## Client Understanding

Everstage has 300+ customers across technology, manufacturing, financial services and pharmaceuticals.

| Buyer | Pain solved |
|---|---|

| Finance | Accurate payouts, audit trail, SOX-ready compliance |
| Sales reps | Real-time earnings visibility, gamification, fewer disputes |
| Sales managers | Team performance dashboards, quota tracking, coaching data |

Financial benefits for clients include commission forecasting and modeling, accurate payout calculations, budgeting and accruals, real-time ROI analysis, detailed analytics, time savings, improved sales performance and reduced dispute rates.
